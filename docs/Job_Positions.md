# Job Positions

## Purpose
Job positions represent operational responsibilities and are distinct from authority roles.

## Examples
- Cleaner
- Laundry Attendant
- Receptionist
- Barman
- Maintenance Technician
- Security Officer

## Behavior
- A user may hold multiple positions.
- Position assignments influence task eligibility, navigation, dashboards, and operational workflows.
- New positions should be added through the database, not hardcoded into templates.
